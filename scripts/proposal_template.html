Summary

Thirteen stores have the worst combination of low miles and high cost per mile among store pickups in 2026. Removing those trucks ends each store's flat pickup billing, and redeploying them to the 2027 lifecycle replacements (including three at Millworks) plus the Region 1, 4 and 6 pickups avoids buying 13 new trucks. Canyon Lake, the new store, would still need a new truck.
