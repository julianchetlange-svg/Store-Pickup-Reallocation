Summary

Eleven stores have a pickup that ranks in both the 15 lowest-mileage and the 15 highest cost-per-mile store pickups for 2026. Removing those trucks ends each store's flat pickup billing, and redeploying them to the 10 stores due for 2027 lifecycle replacement plus the new Canyon Lake store avoids buying 11 new trucks.

1 · Stores losing a pickup — store P&L savings

P&L savings = flat pickup billing of /month no longer charged to the store. Actual expense is shown for reference and excludes fuel, which stays because the work moves to another vehicle.

2 · Truck placement — CAPEX avoided

Replacements are based on mileage: the lowest-odometer trucks go to the highest-usage receiving stores. Each placement avoids a delivered replacement pickup.
